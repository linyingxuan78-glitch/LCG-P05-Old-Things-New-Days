## Short English description (seller page draft, not published)
Run a cosy second-hand shop, one day at a time. Find used goods, organise your shelves, set prices, and welcome shoppers with different needs. Manage daily expenses and stock, deal with the occasional bargain, and make careful choices about the things people bring through your door.

## Features substantiated in the game
- First-person indoor shop with 27 goods across six categories and nine display shelves.
- Shopkeeping loop: source second-hand items, place stock, price items, serve customers, and read daily reports.
- Resident trade-in opportunities, limited supplier availability, customer bargaining and optional community outcomes.
- Seven optional real-life-inspired decisions, including inspecting an appliance, handling a found keepsake, and placing unsold clothes on a free rack—with outcomes that are not always guaranteed.
- Adjustable visual comfort options, picture-supported instructions, optional break reminders, and opt-in **English operating-system speech on the special incident screen** where available.
- Offline single-player by design, with local progress saving.
